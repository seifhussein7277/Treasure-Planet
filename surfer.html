Personal Glider Specifications

JIM'S SOLAR SURFER

Mechanism & Propulsion Mechanics

Jim Hawkins' Solar Surfer is a high-agility personal recreational glider powered by a hybrid propulsion system. It combines a rear liquid-fueled rocket thruster for initial acceleration with a flexible solar sail that collects star radiation currents for steering and speed boosts. Constructed from reinforced light alloys, its dynamic stabilizing fins allow razor-sharp turns and extreme aerodynamic stunts across both atmospheric skies and open cosmic Etherium.

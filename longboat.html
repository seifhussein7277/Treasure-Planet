Secondary ships and boats

Pirate Skiffs and lifeboats' functions

Deployment skiffs and tactical lifeboats serve as the agile secondary fleet aboard larger space galleons. Designed for rapid deployment, these lightweight vessels feature simplified solar sails and auxiliary rocket boosters. Their minimal mass allows for high velocity atmospheric entery and silent orbital approaches. Often utilized by John Silver's crew for surprise boarding actions and landing operations on planetary surfaces, they offer exceptional stealth and speed over heavy starships.

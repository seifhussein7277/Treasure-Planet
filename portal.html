Planetary Portal Core Mechanics

Located at the artificial center of Treasure Planet, the Planetary Portal Core is a colossal interdimensional engine. Controlled directly through Captain Flint's holographic map sphere, the facility features massive rotating concentric rings that bend spatial geometry. When locked onto specific coordinates, the core opens an instantaneous spatial wormhole to any point in the universe, allowing entire starships to transit vast cosmic distances in mere seconds.

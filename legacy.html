THE R.L.S. LEGACY

The R.L.S. Legacy Propulsion & Hull Mechanics

The R.L.S. Legacy is a triple-mastered, solar-powered space galleon engineered for long-range intersteller voyages. Its primary propulsion relies on massive photovoltaic solar sails that harvest cosmic wind and radiation currents in the Etherium. Equiped with an integrated artificial gravity generator and heavy hull plating, the vessel balances classic naval ergonomics with advanced plasma cannon defense systems, making it capable of surviving solar flares, black holes and deep space pirate ambushes.

Character Dossier

"You got the makings of greatness in you, but you got to take the helm and chart your own course!"

Jim Hawkins is a briliiant yet restless teenager from the planet Montressor. After receiving a mysterious sphere from a dying pirate, he discovers the legendary to Treasure Planet. Joining the R.L.S Legacy as a cabin boy, Jim learns life lessons, courage, and leadership through his complex relationship with Long John Silver.
